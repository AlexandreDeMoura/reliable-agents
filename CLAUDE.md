# Reliable Agents Lab

A personal lab for learning to build agent harnesses and reliable agentic workflows. It holds written documents, interactive educational websites for personal use, and case studies.

The foundation is two talks: Basis on reliable agents in production (`basis-talk-transcript.md`, with its slides in `screenshots/`), and an upcoming LangChain talk on when to build your own harness. The project will grow well beyond them.

## Before any task

Read [GLOSSARY.md](GLOSSARY.md) first. It is the project's shared language: write with its terms, and keep the words listed under _Avoid_ for direct quotes from sources. The glossary outranks the term tables inside source transcripts.

When a source or the user uses a word that conflicts with the glossary, raise the conflict before continuing. Record anything left undecided in [docs/open-questions.md](docs/open-questions.md).
